## 1. Purpose

This note preserves a boundary for quantum/entanglement/DNA language when it appears in ONE/RIO/MUSS formation work.

Quantum language may be useful as:

- frontier inquiry
- symbolic pattern language
- metaphor for relational coupling
- prompt for architecture questions
- formation-layer sense-making

It may not be treated as public proof of the architecture without evidence.

Core rule:

```text
Quantum language may serve as formation metaphor or frontier inquiry. It may not become public proof without evidence.
```

---

## 2. Careful distinction

At the molecular level, biology is built on chemistry, and chemistry ultimately obeys quantum physics.

That does not mean that every body-level, nervous-system, or digital-system pattern should be described as literal quantum entanglement.

For ONE/RIO/MUSS, the useful distinction is:

| Register | Allowed use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Molecular science | Quantum effects may be relevant in some biological processes | Do not overgeneralize to whole-system consciousness or architecture proof |
| Symbolic formation | Entanglement/coupling language may point to relationship patterns | Must translate into explicit architecture before build claims |
| Digital architecture | Use event graphs, provenance, APIs, feedback, receipts, and thresholds | Do not claim quantum operation unless implemented and evidenced |
| Public/enterprise | Use evidence-safe language | Avoid unsupported quantum claims |

## 7. Public claim boundary

Do not use quantum language publicly to claim:

- the system is conscious
- the system is alive
- the system is biologically equivalent
- the system has quantum proof
- the system has mystical authority
- the system can bypass ordinary evidence
- the system can infer authority from resonance or correlation

Public claims should use evidence-safe infrastructure language:

- governed AI action
- scoped delegation
- receipt-backed proof
- provenance graph
- policy gate
- prediction governance
- authority boundary
- human-approved consequence
