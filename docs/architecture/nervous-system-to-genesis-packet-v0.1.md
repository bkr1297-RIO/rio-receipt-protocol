```text
Genesis gives prediction a chamber. MANTIS gives prediction a witness. Bondi gives prediction language. RIO gives prediction a boundary. MUSS gives prediction a receipt. The Human Root gives prediction authority — or does not.
```

## 8. Pattern of One patch

The Pattern of One Doctrine prevents coordination from collapsing into totalization.

ONE may provide one workspace, one question, one receipt, one ledger entry, one authority boundary, or one reference point so complexity can become workable.

But no “one” object may erase the plurality it coordinates.

Healthy One preserves difference. Shadow One consumes difference.
