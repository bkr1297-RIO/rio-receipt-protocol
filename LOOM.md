## What this page is

The Loom is a differentiated public estate under assembly. This page is its deliberately small front door: it shows what can be inspected today and the ceiling on what each public organ supports.

An entry is included only when its repository is public and its stated role can be read at the linked source. Inclusion does **not** certify the repository, elevate it into a constitutional office, or imply that it is interoperable with every other entry.

## How to read the estate

Each repository retains its own version, license, change process, and evidence scope. Consult the repository’s current `LICENSE` and README before cloning, reusing, modifying, redistributing, or making claims about it.

The relationship is intentionally differentiated:

```text
protocol and conformance materials
        ↓ define or test named properties
gateway/runtime implementation     typed intake boundary
        ↓                              ↓
receipt and local conformance evidence
        ↓
scoped return of proof
```

This diagram is an orientation map, not a declaration that all repositories are presently wired together or that any single repository exercises authority on behalf of another.

## What is intentionally absent

No private repository, candidate architecture document, unverified deployment, credentialed connector, human-approval service, or research artifact is linked here. A repository becomes eligible only after its public source, role, and claim ceiling are independently reviewed and recorded.

Recent Forge packets such as CTG-P5, CTCL-METRO, Pattern Kernel, and P16 Master Seed falsification remain off-index until a public conformance repository owner, release posture, and claim ceiling are chosen. Their local fixture results should not be inferred from this public Loom page.
